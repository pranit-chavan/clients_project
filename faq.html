Frequently Asked

Everything you may want to know about our eggs, feed, delivery and pricing.

Are the eggs 100% natural?

Yes. Our hens eat only natural, balanced feed — 100% natural food, no shortcuts and nothing artificial.

What makes your eggs “premium”?

Freshness with proof. Every egg is collected, graded and checked by hand, gently cleaned the natural way, and stamped only when it passes every safety check.

Do you follow NECC standards?

Yes — NECC standard compliance. Eggs are graded and priced on the daily NECC rate, so there are no surprises for you.

What pack sizes are available?

Pack of 6, pack of 12, and a tray of 30. Businesses can ask for daily scheduled supply.

Do you deliver to homes?

Yes — from farm to homes and businesses, with same-day delivery in our areas. WhatsApp us your locality to confirm.

Do you supply outside Nashik?

Yes, we currently supply to 2+ states. WhatsApp us to check whether we reach your area.

Do you supply to hotels, canteens and shops?

Yes — 10+ businesses already rely on us for daily egg supply. Ask for a bulk quote on WhatsApp and we will plan a fixed schedule for you.

How is your poultry cared for?

Every partner farm works under veterinary guidance. A doctor oversees poultry care across our network of 45+ farms.
